Liked Songs, Sorted

Built from your liked songs only. Your old playlists weren't used at all. Every song is placed by its main artist's sound, so genres that were mixed together before are now split apart. Duplicate versions of the same song are removed. Songs marked New for you are picks you don't have yet, and each card also lists more suggestions to look up on Spotify.

Adding a playlist in Spotify

  1. In the Spotify desktop app, create a playlist with the name shown. These names don't match your old playlists, so you can keep or delete those separately.
  2. Click Copy song links on that card below.
  3. Click inside the empty playlist and paste (Ctrl+V / ⌘V). All the songs get added at once.
